# Hypertrophy Program

A 4-day upper/lower hypertrophy block built around the principle that **weekly volume per muscle group** is the primary driver of growth. Heavy sets build tension, moderate sets build tension + metabolic stress, and light sets push blood and metabolites. All three matter.

## Overview

This is an 8-week block designed for athletes who want to add muscle with minimal fat gain. It assumes a base of strength training and established working weights. Sessions are organized upper/lower, run twice per week, with progressive volume increases across the block.

**Block structure:**

- Weeks 1–3: Base volume, moderate intensity
- Week 4: Deload (50% volume, keep intensity)
- Weeks 5–7: Peak volume, add one set per exercise
- Week 8: Deload + testing

## Who It Is For

- Athletes who want to add lean mass
- Anyone who's finished a linear strength program and wants to shift focus
- Athletes in an off-season or general prep phase
- Anyone returning after a break who wants to rebuild

**Not for:**
- Beginners (do Starting Strength or StrongLifts first)
- Athletes 2 weeks out from competition
- Anyone in a significant calorie deficit

## Weekly Structure

Four days per week, upper/lower split:

| Day | Focus | Muscle Groups |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Upper A | Chest, back, shoulders, arms |
| Tuesday | Lower A | Quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves |
| Thursday | Upper B | Back, chest, shoulders, arms |
| Friday | Lower B | Posterior chain, quads, glutes, calves |

Wednesday, Saturday, Sunday are rest days.

**Optional 5th day:** arms or weak points, low volume.

## Sample Session — Upper A

1. **Barbell Bench Press** — 4×6–8 @ RPE 8
2. **Bent-Over Row** — 4×8–10 @ RPE 8
3. **Incline Dumbbell Press** — 3×10–12 @ RPE 8
4. **Lat Pulldown** — 3×10–12 @ RPE 8
5. **Lateral Raise** — 3×12–15 @ RPE 9
6. **Triceps Pushdown** — 3×10–15 @ RPE 9
7. **Bicep Curl** — 3×10–15 @ RPE 9

## Sample Session — Lower A

1. **Back Squat** — 4×6–8 @ RPE 8
2. **Romanian Deadlift** — 4×8–10 @ RPE 8
3. **Leg Press** — 3×10–12 @ RPE 8
4. **Leg Curl** — 3×10–12 @ RPE 9
5. **Calf Raise** — 4×12–15 @ RPE 9
6. **Hanging Knee Raise** — 3×12–15

## Progression Rules

**Within a session:** Add reps first, then weight.

- Week 1: 4×6 at RPE 8
- Week 2: 4×7 at same weight
- Week 3: 4×8 at same weight
- Week 4 (deload): 2×6 at same weight
- Week 5: Add 5–10 lbs, back to 4×6
- Repeat

**Across the block:** Add one working set per exercise each 3-week cycle. By week 7 you're doing 5–6 sets per exercise on the main lifts.

## Volume Targets

**Per muscle group per week:**

- Minimum: 10 hard sets
- Target: 14–20 hard sets
- Advanced: 20–25 hard sets (only if recovery is elite)

Count a set as "hard" if it's taken to RPE 7 or higher (3 or fewer reps left in reserve).

## Autoregulation in Practice

This is a **hypertrophy block**, not a strength peak. RPE targets are 7–9, not 9–10.

- **RPE 7:** 3 reps in reserve — leave it there on weeks 1–3
- **RPE 8:** 2 reps in reserve — the working sweet spot
- **RPE 9:** 1 rep in reserve — last set of each exercise, occasionally
- **RPE 10:** failure — rarely, and only on isolation exercises

If your RPE is running 2+ points above target for 3+ sessions, cut the volume 20–30% and reassess sleep and nutrition.

## Nutrition & Recovery

Hypertrophy requires fuel. This program will not work in a deficit.

- **Calories:** 200–400 above maintenance
- **Protein:** 1.6–2.2 g/kg per day
- **Sleep:** 7–9 hours
- **Deload:** every 4 weeks, no exceptions

## Deload Protocol

**Week 4 and week 8:**

- Cut volume 50–60%
- Keep intensity (same weights, half the sets)
- Keep frequency (same days)
- RPE 5–7

The deload is not optional. It's when the adaptation happens.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Chasing weight instead of reps.** Reps first, weight second.
- **Skipping the deload.** You will stall and regress.
- **Too much cardio.** 2× per week max, low intensity.
- **Not eating enough.** Hypertrophy without a surplus is a slow grind.
- **Comparing sessions.** Some days are stronger. Trust the weekly trend, not the daily number.

## When to Switch

- **After 8 weeks:** deload, retest working weights, then either repeat the block or shift to a strength phase (Wendler 5/3/1, Texas Method).
- **If you stall for 3 weeks:** switch to a different split or reduce volume.
- **If you're approaching competition:** shift to a sport-specific or power block.
