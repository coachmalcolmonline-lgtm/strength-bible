# Sport-Specific Program

An off-season and in-season template for team sport athletes. Built on the principle that strength is the base, power is the application, and in-season training is maintenance, not development.

## Overview

This program is a framework, not a fixed routine. It adapts to:

- The sport and position
- The phase of the season
- The athlete's current strength level

The goal is not to build the strongest athlete in the weight room. The goal is to build the strongest, fastest, most powerful athlete on the field.

**Phases:**

- **Off-season (12-16 weeks):** Build general strength, size, and work capacity
- **Pre-season (4-6 weeks):** Develop sport-specific power, speed, agility
- **In-season (season length):** Maintain strength with minimal volume
- **Post-season (2-4 weeks):** Rest, recover, rebuild

## Who It Is For

- Team sport athletes (football, basketball, soccer, lacrosse, hockey, etc.)
- Individual sport athletes (baseball, track, swimming, wrestling)
- High school, college, or adult athletes with a competitive season

**Not for:**
- Beginners (build a strength base first with Starting Strength or StrongLifts)
- Off-season athletes with no competitive season -- use a strength or hypertrophy block instead

## The Framework

### Off-Season (12-16 Weeks)

**Goal:** build the biggest general base possible.

**Weekly structure:** 4 days per week, upper/lower split.

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | Lower + power |
| Tue | Upper + grip |
| Wed | Rest / mobility |
| Thu | Lower + speed |
| Fri | Upper + core |

**Main lifts:** Back squat, front squat, deadlift, press, bench, Olympic derivatives

**Volume:** 4-6 sets per lift, RPE 7-8

**Accessory work:** carries, sled work, unilateral work

**Speed/agility:** 2x per week, low volume, high quality

**Conditioning:** 2-3x per week, aerobic base

### Pre-Season (4-6 Weeks)

**Goal:** convert strength into sport-specific power and speed.

**Weekly structure:** 3-4 days per week, mixed.

**Main lifts:** same as off-season, reduced volume (3-4 sets), higher intensity (RPE 8)

**Power work:** Olympic derivatives, jumps, throws -- increased volume

**Speed/agility:** 3x per week, sport-specific drills

**Conditioning:** 2x per week, sport-specific intervals

### In-Season

**Goal:** maintain strength, stay healthy, don't add fatigue.

**Weekly structure:** 2 days per week, 45-60 min sessions.

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | Lower + core |
| Tue | Rest |
| Wed | Upper + power |
| Thu | Rest |
| Fri | Practice or game |
| Sat | Game |
| Sun | Rest |

**Main lifts:**

- Back squat: 2-3 sets x 3-5 @ 80-85%
- Deadlift: 2-3 sets x 3-5 @ 80-85%
- Press: 2-3 sets x 3-5 @ 80-85%
- Olympic derivative: 3-4 sets x 2-3 @ 70-80%

**Accessory work:** 2-3 exercises, low volume

**No soreness-inducing work.** Never train to failure.

### Post-Season (2-4 Weeks)

**Goal:** rest and rebuild.

**Activity:** light training, mobility, general physical preparedness

**No heavy lifting.** Focus on sleep, food, and mental recovery.

## Sport-Specific Examples

### Football -- Lineman

**Off-season:** heavy strength focus. Back squat, deadlift, front squat, press, bench. Low aerobic volume. High caloric intake to support mass.

**Pre-season:** heavy strength + explosive power. Add sled pushes, sled drags, med ball throws. Speed work minimal (linemen don't sprint far).

**In-season:** 2x per week maintenance. Squat, deadlift, press. No conditioning beyond practice.

### Football -- Skill Position

**Off-season:** strength base + speed development. Back squat, deadlift, power clean. Sprint work 2x per week. Moderate aerobic base.

**Pre-season:** speed emphasis. Sprints, plyometrics, change-of-direction drills. Strength maintained.

**In-season:** 2x per week maintenance. Focus on speed and health.

### Basketball

**Off-season:** strength + vertical jump development. Back squat, trap bar deadlift, front squat, Romanian deadlift. Jump work 2x per week. Moderate conditioning.

**Pre-season:** jump + speed. Plyometrics, sprints, change of direction. Strength maintained.

**In-season:** 2x per week maintenance. Focus on squats, single-leg work, core.

### Baseball / Softball

**Off-season:** strength + rotational power. Back squat, deadlift, bench, row. Add med ball throws, rotational work.

**Pre-season:** rotational power + arm health. Band work, med ball throws, sprint work. Strength maintained.

**In-season:** 2x per week maintenance. Focus on core, arm care, lower body.

**Pitcher-specific:** shoulder health is critical. Never train through shoulder pain. Rotator cuff and scapular work every session.

### Soccer / Field Hockey

**Off-season:** strength + aerobic base. Back squat, deadlift, hip thrust. Long runs and tempo runs.

**Pre-season:** speed + sport-specific conditioning. Sprints, change of direction, interval work.

**In-season:** 2x per week maintenance. Focus on lower body strength, hip stability.

### Wrestling

**Off-season:** strength + power. Back squat, deadlift, power clean, press. Moderate weight management.

**Pre-season:** strength maintained, conditioning emphasized. Sport-specific drills, grip work.

**In-season:** 2x per week maintenance. Focus on strength preservation during weight cutting. Never cut weight while training heavy.

## Progression Rules

**Off-season:**
- Add 5-10 lbs per week to main lifts
- Add 1-2 reps per set when close to top of rep range
- Add volume only every 3-4 weeks

**Pre-season:**
- Maintain main lift loads
- Increase power work volume
- Increase speed work intensity

**In-season:**
- Hold the weights steady. Do not chase PRs.
- If a lift stalls, drop 5-10% and hold

## Deload Protocol

**Off-season:** deload every 4-6 weeks (cut volume 40-60%)

**Pre-season:** deload 2 weeks before season starts

**In-season:** deload every 4-6 weeks (cut volume 40%, keep intensity)

**Post-season:** full rest for 2-4 weeks

## Common Pitfalls

- **Training too hard in-season.** The season is not the time to build. Maintain.
- **Skipping off-season work.** This is when you actually get stronger.
- **No sport-specific speed work.** Strength doesn't transfer to speed without speed training.
- **Ignoring position differences.** A lineman and a receiver need different programs.
- **Training through pain.** In-season athletes must protect their health first.
- **No sleep.** Everything else is rounding error if you don't sleep.

## Testing

Test at the start and end of each phase:

| Test | Metric |
|---|---|
| Back squat | 3RM or 5RM |
| Deadlift | 3RM or 5RM |
| Press | 5RM |
| Vertical jump | Height |
| Broad jump | Distance |
| 10m sprint | Time |
| 30m sprint | Time |
| 5-10-5 shuttle | Time |

Improvements of 5-10% in any of these over 12 weeks are meaningful.

## When to Switch

- **After off-season:** transition to pre-season
- **After pre-season:** transition to in-season maintenance
- **After in-season:** post-season rest, then repeat
