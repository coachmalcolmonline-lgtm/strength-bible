# Conditioning Program

An 8-week conditioning block that develops all three energy systems without killing your strength. Built for team-sport athletes and general fitness athletes who need an engine, not just a chassis.

## Overview

Two phases, four weeks each. Phase 1 builds the aerobic base with mostly easy work. Phase 2 adds intensity, sharpening the ATP-PC and glycolytic systems.

**Block structure:**

- Weeks 1-4: Aerobic base + threshold work
- Week 5: Deload
- Weeks 6-7: HIIT + sprint sharpening
- Week 8: Test week + taper

## Who It Is For

- Team sport athletes (football, soccer, basketball, lacrosse, hockey)
- General fitness athletes who want an engine
- Strength athletes adding conditioning during an off-season block
- Anyone returning from a break who wants to rebuild work capacity

**Not for:**
- Pure powerlifters or Olympic lifters in a strength peak
- Anyone in-season with high game volume
- Beginners (build a strength base first)

## Weekly Structure

Depends on goal:

**Option A -- Team sport athlete (3 conditioning sessions/week):**

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | Strength + 20 min LISS |
| Tue | Intervals (glycolytic) |
| Wed | LISS (30 min Zone 2) |
| Thu | Strength + 15 min LISS |
| Fri | Sprint intervals (ATP-PC) |
| Sat | Sport practice or off |
| Sun | Off |

**Option B -- Strength athlete adding conditioning (2 sessions/week):**

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | Heavy lower + 15 min LISS |
| Tue | Upper |
| Wed | LISS (40 min Zone 2) |
| Thu | Heavy lower |
| Fri | Upper + 15 min intervals |
| Sat | Off |
| Sun | Off |

## Phase 1 -- Aerobic Base (Weeks 1-4)

**Goal:** build the aerobic engine. 80% easy, 20% moderate.

**Week 1:**
- 3x LISS: 30 min Zone 2
- 1x Tempo: 3x8 min at Zone 3, 2 min rest

**Week 2:**
- 3x LISS: 35 min Zone 2
- 1x Tempo: 4x8 min at Zone 3, 2 min rest

**Week 3:**
- 3x LISS: 40 min Zone 2
- 1x Tempo: 3x10 min at Zone 3-4, 2 min rest

**Week 4:**
- 2x LISS: 40 min Zone 2
- 1x Tempo: 2x10 min Zone 3

**Week 5 -- Deload:**
- 2x LISS: 30 min Zone 2, easy

## Phase 2 -- Sharpening (Weeks 6-7)

**Goal:** develop VO2max, glycolytic capacity, and short-burst power.

**Week 6:**
- 2x LISS: 30 min Zone 2 (recovery)
- 1x HIIT: 4x4 min at 90-95% max HR, 3 min active rest
- 1x Sprints: 6x10 sec max effort, 3-5 min rest

**Week 7:**
- 2x LISS: 30 min Zone 2
- 1x HIIT: 6x2 min at 90% max HR, 2 min rest
- 1x Sprints: 8x10 sec max effort, 3-5 min rest

**Week 8 -- Taper + Test:**
- 1x LISS: 20 min easy
- Test day: see Testing Battery below

## The 80/20 Rule

80% of your conditioning volume should be **easy** (Zone 1-2, conversational). 20% should be **hard** (Zone 4-5).

Most athletes do the opposite -- they train moderately hard all the time. That's the worst of both worlds: not hard enough to drive adaptation, not easy enough to recover.

Follow the 80/20. It works.

## Testing Battery

Test at week 1 and week 8.

- **2-mile run** (aerobic)
- **500m row or 1-min bike for distance** (anaerobic)
- **5-min bike for max distance** (VO2max proxy)
- **Resting heart rate** (morning, fasted)
- **HRV** (via app if available)

Track all five. Improvements across the board = the block worked.

## Recovery & Nutrition

- **Sleep:** 7-9 hours. Non-negotiable.
- **Protein:** 1.6-2.2 g/kg per day.
- **Carbs:** higher on hard days, lower on easy days.
- **Water:** 3+ liters on training days. Add electrolytes on hot days.

## Deload Protocol

**Week 5:**
- Cut conditioning volume 40-50%
- Keep intensity low (Zone 2 only)
- No intervals or sprints
- One light tempo session max

**Week 8:**
- Taper before test day
- No hard sessions within 48 hours of testing

## Common Pitfalls

- **Too much moderate-intensity work.** Zone 3 all the time is the worst zone to live in.
- **Skipping the aerobic base.** You can't sharpen what doesn't exist.
- **Stacking hard conditioning on hard lifting.** Separate by 6+ hours or different days.
- **Sprinting when tired.** Hamstring tears happen here.
- **Ignoring heart rate.** Zone 2 should feel easy. If you can't hold a conversation, you're going too hard.

## When to Switch

- **After 8 weeks:** retest, reassess goals. Repeat or shift to strength focus.
- **In-season:** cut to 2x/week maintenance (1 LISS + 1 interval).
- **Peak strength phase:** minimum 1x/week LISS only.

## Journal

Uses the **Training Log -- Conditioning** template. Track session type, duration, HR zone, RPE, and how you felt.
