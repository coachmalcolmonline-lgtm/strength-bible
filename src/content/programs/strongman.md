# Strongman Program

An 8-week strongman block that develops grip, trunk, and full-body power using loaded carries, sled work, and odd objects. Built for athletes who want real-world strength that transfers beyond the barbell.

## Overview

Strongman training is a different beast from powerlifting or weightlifting. Instead of three fixed lifts, you train a variety of events -- carries, drags, flips, loads -- each with different demands. The program is organized around four pillars:

- **Grip:** farmer carries, dead hangs, plate pinches
- **Trunk:** yoke walks, heavy carries, front-loaded work
- **Hip power:** sled pushes, tire flips, stone loads
- **Overall conditioning:** medley events, time-based carries

**Block structure:**

- Weeks 1-4: Skill acquisition + base loading
- Week 5: Deload
- Weeks 6-7: Heavy singles and event-specific work
- Week 8: Test / event day

**Weekly structure:** 4 sessions per week, 60-75 min each.

## Who It Is For

- Athletes who want strongman-specific development
- General strength athletes who want to add real-world capacity
- Team sport athletes in an off-season block
- Anyone who wants to train grip, trunk, and power together

**Not for:**
- Beginners (build a strength base first with Starting Strength or StrongLifts)
- Athletes without access to strongman implements
- Athletes in-season with high game volume
- Anyone with active lower-back or shoulder injuries

## Weekly Structure

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Monday | Lower body + Sled work |
| Tuesday | Upper body + Grip |
| Wednesday | Rest / mobility |
| Thursday | Events day (yoke, stones, carries) |
| Friday | Full body + Heavy carries |
| Saturday | Rest or LISS |
| Sunday | Rest |

**Total weekly volume:** ~4 hours of training, split into 4 sessions.

## Sample Week -- Block 1 (Weeks 1-4)

**Monday -- Lower + Sled:**
- Back squat: 4x5 @ 80%
- Romanian deadlift: 3x8 @ 70%
- Sled push: 6x20m at moderate load, 90 sec rest
- Leg curl: 3x10

**Tuesday -- Upper + Grip:**
- Bench press: 4x5 @ 80%
- Barbell row: 4x6 @ 75%
- Farmer carry: 4x30m at moderate load, 90 sec rest
- Plate pinch: 3x30 sec per hand
- Dead hang: 3x max time

**Thursday -- Events Day:**
- Yoke walk: 4x15m at moderate load, 2 min rest
- Atlas stone load to platform: 5x3 at moderate load, 2 min rest
- Sandbag carry: 4x20m
- Tire flip: 4x4

**Friday -- Full Body + Heavy Carries:**
- Deadlift: 4x3 @ 85%
- Overhead press: 4x5 @ 80%
- Suitcase carry: 4x30m per side
- Sled drag (backward): 4x20m
- Ab wheel: 3x10

## Sample Week -- Block 2 (Weeks 6-7)

**Monday -- Lower + Sled:**
- Back squat: 5x3 @ 85-90%
- Sled push: 8x20m at heavy load, 2 min rest

**Tuesday -- Upper + Grip:**
- Bench press: 5x3 @ 85%
- Farmer carry: 4x20m at heavy load, 2 min rest
- Plate pinch: 3x20 sec per hand, heavy

**Thursday -- Events Day:**
- Yoke walk: 4x15m at heavy load
- Atlas stone load: 5x1 at heavy load
- Sandbag carry: 4x15m heavy
- Tire flip: 4x6

**Friday -- Full Body + Medley:**
- Deadlift: 4x2 @ 90%
- Overhead press: 4x3 @ 85%
- Medley: sled push 20m + farmer carry 20m + yoke 15m + stone load x2, 3 rounds

## Progression Rules

- **Load:** add 5-10% per week to each main lift or event
- **Distance:** add 5m per week to carries
- **Reps:** add 1-2 reps per set before increasing load
- **Never** add both load and volume in the same week

## Event Priorities

Match your training to your goal:

| Goal | Focus |
|---|---|
| General strength | Farmer carries, sled work, heavy deadlifts |
| Grip emphasis | Farmer carries, dead hangs, plate pinches |
| Trunk emphasis | Yoke walks, front-loaded carries, overhead work |
| Hip power | Sled pushes, tire flips, stone loads |
| Competition prep | All events, medley work, timed sets |

## Recovery & Nutrition

Strongman training is the highest structural load in the weight room.

- **Sleep:** 8-9 hours
- **Protein:** 1.8-2.2 g/kg per day
- **Calories:** at maintenance or slight surplus
- **Water:** 3+ liters on training days
- **Belt:** use for heavy yoke walks and deadlifts above 85%
- **Chalk:** liberally, especially for carries and stones
- **Rest between events:** 2-3 min

## Deload Protocol

**Week 5:**

- Cut volume 40%
- Keep intensity moderate (70-75%)
- Focus on technique and grip maintenance
- No heavy singles or event attempts
- Sleep 8+ hours per night

## Common Pitfalls

- **Too much volume too fast.** Strongman punishes overtrained athletes.
- **Skipping the warm-up.** Yoke and stone work demand full mobility.
- **Poor grip training.** Grip failure ends carries early.
- **Ignoring technique on carries.** Bad posture is a spine injury waiting to happen.
- **Training events when tired.** Events demand fresh CNS.
- **No deload.** Structural adaptations need time; the deload is not optional.

## Testing

Test at week 1 and week 8:

| Test | Metric |
|---|---|
| Farmer carry | Max load for 30m |
| Yoke walk | Max load for 15m |
| Atlas stone | Max load to 48" |
| Sled push | Time for 20m at fixed load |
| Tire flip | Max flips in 60 sec |
| Deadlift | 1RM or 3RM |

## When to Switch

- **After 8 weeks:** retest, then either repeat with new loads or shift to a strength or sport-specific block.
- **In-season:** drop to 2x per week maintenance (1 carry + 1 medley).
- **Off-season:** this block stacks well with hypertrophy or strength work.
