# Basketball Program

An off-season and in-season template for basketball athletes. Builds strength, vertical jump, change of direction, and conditioning -- then maintains them through a long season.

## Overview

Basketball demands jumping, cutting, sprinting, and landing -- over and over, for 32-40 minutes, 30+ games per season. This program builds the physical base to do all of that, and the durability to survive the season.

**Phases:**

- **Off-season (12-16 weeks):** Build strength, jump, speed
- **Pre-season (4-6 weeks):** Sharpen jump and cut
- **In-season (season length):** Maintain with minimal volume
- **Post-season (2-4 weeks):** Rest, recover

## Who It Is For

- High school, college, or adult basketball players
- Both male and female athletes (with ACL-prevention emphasis for females)
- Any position -- program adapts by position needs

**Not for:**
- Beginners (build a strength base first with Starting Strength or StrongLifts)
- Athletes with active knee, ankle, or hip injuries

## Position-Specific Emphasis

| Position | Emphasis |
|---|---|
| Point guard | Acceleration, change of direction, quickness |
| Shooting guard | Speed, jump, shooting stamina |
| Small forward | Balanced -- strength, speed, jump |
| Power forward | Strength, jump, physical play |
| Center | Max strength, jump, landing, body mass |

## Off-Season (Weeks 1-12)

**Goal:** build strength, jump, and speed.

**Weekly structure:** 4 training days + 2 skill days

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | Lower body + power (jumps) |
| Tue | Upper body + skill work |
| Wed | Rest / mobility |
| Thu | Lower body + sprints |
| Fri | Upper body + conditioning |
| Sat | Skill work / open gym |
| Sun | Rest |

**Monday -- Lower + Power:**
- Back squat: 4x5 @ 80%
- Trap bar deadlift: 3x5 @ 80%
- Box jumps: 4x5 (step down)
- Broad jumps: 3x5
- Nordic curl: 3x5
- Calf raise: 3x12

**Tuesday -- Upper + Skill:**
- Bench press: 4x5 @ 80%
- Barbell row: 4x6 @ 75%
- Overhead press: 3x6 @ 75%
- Pull-up: 3x6
- Med ball throws: 3x5 each (chest, overhead)

**Thursday -- Lower + Sprint:**
- Front squat: 4x5 @ 75%
- Romanian deadlift: 3x8 @ 70%
- 10m sprints: 8x, full recovery
- 30m sprints: 4x, full recovery
- Change of direction drills: 4 sets

**Friday -- Upper + Conditioning:**
- Incline press: 3x8 @ 75%
- Dumbbell row: 3x8
- Face pulls: 3x15
- Repeated sprint conditioning: 8x30 sec hard / 60 sec easy

## Pre-Season (Weeks 13-16)

**Goal:** sharpen jump and cut, maintain strength.

**Weekly structure:** 3-4 lifts + sport-specific work

- Reduce main lift volume to 3 sets
- Keep intensity (RPE 8)
- Increase plyometric work
- Increase change-of-direction work
- Sport-specific conditioning: full-court drills, sprint-jog-sprint sequences

## In-Season

**Goal:** maintain strength and jump, stay healthy.

**Weekly structure:** 2 lifts + practices/games

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | Full body (low volume) + jumps |
| Tue | Practice / game |
| Wed | Recovery + skill work |
| Thu | Full body (low volume) |
| Fri | Game |
| Sat | Game |
| Sun | Rest |

**Monday -- Full Body A:**
- Back squat: 2x3 @ 85%
- Trap bar deadlift: 2x3 @ 85%
- Box jumps: 3x3
- Nordic curl: 2x5

**Thursday -- Full Body B:**
- Front squat: 2x3 @ 85%
- Bench press: 2x3 @ 85%
- Pull-up: 2x5
- Single-leg work: 2x8

**Rules:**
- 45 min max per session
- No soreness-inducing work
- No max jumps in-season -- moderate intensity only
- Landing mechanics every session
- Sleep 8-9 hours

## Jump Training

Vertical jump is the signature basketball quality. Train it consistently.

**Off-season:**
- 2-3 jump sessions per week
- 60-120 ground contacts per week
- Mix of box jumps, broad jumps, depth jumps, single-leg jumps
- Progress intensity slowly

**Pre-season:**
- 2 jump sessions per week
- 60-80 ground contacts
- More max-effort jumps

**In-season:**
- 1-2 jump sessions per week
- 20-40 ground contacts
- Maintenance only

**Landing mechanics every jump session:**
- Land soft, quiet, on mid-foot
- Knees track over toes (no valgus collapse)
- Hip hinge on landing
- Reset between reps

## ACL Prevention (Especially for Female Athletes)

Female basketball athletes have 3-6x higher ACL injury rates. This program includes prevention work every session:

- **Single-leg strength:** split squats, single-leg RDL, single-leg glute bridges
- **Nordic curls:** hamstring eccentrics 2-3x per week
- **Landing drills:** depth drops, box jump landings
- **Lateral band walks:** hip abductor strength
- **Balance work:** single-leg balance, eyes-closed progressions

Never skip these. They are not optional.

## Progression Rules

**Off-season:**
- Add 5-10 lbs per week to main lifts
- Add jump volume every 2-3 weeks
- Add sprint volume every 2 weeks
- Deload every 6-8 weeks

**Pre-season:**
- Maintain main lift loads
- Increase jump and COD specificity
- Deload 2 weeks before season

**In-season:**
- Hold the weights steady. No PRs.
- Deload every 4-6 weeks (cut volume 40%, keep intensity)

## Testing

Test at start and end of off-season, and mid-season:

| Test | Metric |
|---|---|
| Vertical jump | Height |
| Standing broad jump | Distance |
| 10m sprint | Time |
| 30m sprint | Time |
| 5-10-5 shuttle | Time |
| Back squat | 3RM |
| Trap bar deadlift | 3RM |
| Pull-up | Max reps |

## Common Pitfalls

- **Training too hard in-season.** The season is long. Conserve energy.
- **No jump training.** Vertical jump is trainable. Train it.
- **No landing mechanics work.** This is how ACL injuries happen.
- **Skipping single-leg work.** Basketball is a single-leg sport.
- **Too much conditioning.** Basketball isn't cross country. Repeated sprint training is enough.
- **Maxing out in-season.** Never do this.

## When to Switch

- **After off-season:** transition to pre-season
- **After pre-season:** transition to in-season
- **After in-season:** 2-4 weeks of rest, then off-season
