# Flag Football Program

An off-season and in-season template for flag football athletes. Speed-first, lean-focused, no collision prep.

## Overview

Flag football is not tackle football. Without collisions, linemen, or mass-building demands, it's closer to a speed-and-agility sport. This program reflects that.

**Phases:**

- **Off-season (12-16 weeks):** Build speed, power, and lean strength
- **Pre-season (4-6 weeks):** Sharpen speed, COD, and sport-specific conditioning
- **In-season (season length):** Maintain speed and strength, prevent injury
- **Post-season (2-4 weeks):** Rest

## Who It Is For

- High school, college, or adult flag football players
- All positions -- but speed is the shared priority
- Athletes transitioning from tackle football (drop the neck work, add conditioning)

**Not for:**
- Beginners (build a strength base first)
- Athletes with active hamstring, ankle, or knee injuries

## Position-Specific Emphasis

| Position | Emphasis |
|---|---|
| Rusher | Acceleration, change of direction, agility |
| Receiver | Acceleration, top speed, cutting |
| Running back | Acceleration, power, cutting |
| Safety / DB | Backpedal, break on ball, COD |
| Center | Snap accuracy, quickness, route running |
| Quarterback | Arm strength, mobility, decision-making |

## Off-Season (Weeks 1-12)

**Goal:** build speed, power, lean strength.

**Weekly structure:** 4 lifts + 3 speed/conditioning days

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | Lower body + speed |
| Tue | Conditioning + agility |
| Wed | Rest / mobility |
| Thu | Speed + COD |
| Fri | Upper body + power |
| Sat | Skill work / open play |
| Sun | Rest |

**Monday -- Lower + Speed:**
- Back squat: 4x5 @ 80%
- Romanian deadlift: 3x8 @ 70%
- Bulgarian split squat: 3x8 each leg
- 10m sprints: 8x, full recovery
- Nordic curl: 3x5

**Tuesday -- Conditioning + Agility:**
- Small-sided games or repeated sprint intervals: 8x30 sec hard / 60 sec easy
- 5-10-5 shuttle: 6 sets
- Pro-agility shuttle: 6 sets
- Lateral shuttle: 6 sets

**Thursday -- Speed + COD:**
- 30m sprints: 6x, full recovery
- 40yd sprints: 4x, full recovery
- L-drill: 6 sets
- Cut drills: 6 sets
- Box jumps: 4x5

**Friday -- Upper + Power:**
- Bench press: 4x5 @ 80%
- Barbell row: 4x6 @ 75%
- Overhead press: 3x6 @ 75%
- Pull-up: 3x6
- Med ball throws: 3x5 each (chest, overhead, rotational)

## Pre-Season (Weeks 13-16)

**Goal:** sharpen speed and COD, maintain strength.

- Reduce main lift volume to 3 sets
- Keep intensity (RPE 8)
- Increase speed and COD work
- Sport-specific conditioning: small-sided games
- Deload 2 weeks before season

## In-Season

**Goal:** maintain speed and strength, prevent injury.

**Weekly structure:** 2 lifts + practices/games

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | Full body (low volume) + speed |
| Tue | Practice / game |
| Wed | Recovery + mobility |
| Thu | Full body (low volume) |
| Fri | Game |
| Sat | Game |
| Sun | Rest |

**Monday -- Full Body + Speed:**
- Back squat: 2x3 @ 85%
- Trap bar deadlift: 2x3 @ 85%
- 10m sprints: 4x, full recovery
- Nordic curl: 2x5

**Thursday -- Full Body B:**
- Bench press: 2x3 @ 85%
- Overhead press: 2x3 @ 80%
- Pull-up: 2x5
- Single-leg work: 2x8
- Copenhagen plank: 2x20 sec

**Rules:**
- 45 min max per session
- No soreness-inducing work
- No max sprints in-season -- practice provides that
- Nordic curls every week
- Single-leg balance every week
- Sleep 8-9 hours

## Speed Training Focus

Speed is the #1 quality for flag football. Do not skip it.

**Off-season:**
- 3-4 speed sessions per week
- 2 sessions with acceleration focus (10-20m)
- 1-2 sessions with max velocity (30-40m)
- Full recovery between sprints

**Pre-season:**
- 2-3 speed sessions per week
- More max-effort work

**In-season:**
- 1-2 speed sessions per week
- Maintenance only

**Never train speed when tired.** Speed is a CNS quality and requires fresh legs.

## Injury Prevention (Every Session)

Even without collisions, flag football has notable injury rates from sprinting and cutting.

- **Nordic curls:** 2-3x per week (hamstring)
- **Single-leg balance:** 3x per week (ankle)
- **Copenhagen planks:** 2-3x per week (groin)
- **Lateral band walks:** 2-3x per week (hip stability)
- **Landing mechanics:** 1-2x per week (ACL)
- **Hip flexor mobility:** daily
- **Ankle mobility:** daily

## Progression Rules

**Off-season:**
- Add 5-10 lbs per week to main lifts
- Add 5% per week to speed volume
- Deload every 6-8 weeks

**Pre-season:**
- Maintain main lift loads
- Increase speed and COD specificity
- Deload 2 weeks before season

**In-season:**
- Hold the weights steady. No PRs.
- Deload every 4-6 weeks

## Body Composition

Flag football rewards a lean, powerful build. Unlike tackle football, extra mass slows you down.

- Target body fat: 8-15% (males), 15-22% (females)
- Prioritize power-to-weight ratio
- Don't chase mass
- Eat enough protein (1.6-2.2 g/kg)
- Calories at maintenance or slight surplus during off-season

## Common Pitfalls

- **Training like tackle football.** No neck work, no mass building, no lineman strength.
- **Skipping speed work.** Speed is the sport.
- **Not enough conditioning.** Flag football has more continuous movement than tackle.
- **Getting heavy.** Extra mass = slower. Stay lean.
- **No Nordic curls.** Hamstring injuries are the #1 soft tissue injury.
- **Maxing out in-season.** Never do this.

## When to Switch

- **After off-season:** transition to pre-season
- **After pre-season:** transition to in-season
- **After in-season:** 2-4 weeks of rest, then off-season
