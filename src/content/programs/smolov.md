# Smolov

Smolov is a 13-week squat specialization program developed by Sergey Smolov.
It is one of the most demanding strength programs in existence. Athletes run
it to add 30-100+ lbs to their squat in 13 weeks.

It is not a beginner program. It is not sustainable year-round. It is a tool
for breaking through a squat plateau.

## Overview

Smolov is divided into three phases:

1. **Base Mesocycle (Weeks 1-4):** High-frequency, high-volume squat work. 4 sessions per week.
2. **Switching Phase (Weeks 5-6):** Reduced frequency, moderate intensity.
3. **Intense Mesocycle (Weeks 7-12):** Peak intensity work. 3 sessions per week.
4. **Taper (Week 13):** Week of rest and testing.

Some athletes run only the Base Mesocycle (4 weeks) and call it Smolov Jr.
(followed by a second cycle if needed).

## Who It Is For

Smolov is for experienced lifters with 12+ months of training, a solid squat
1RM, and the recovery capacity to handle 4 squat sessions per week. It is
not for beginners. It is not for athletes juggling other high-volume training.

## Prerequisites

1. Complete all foundational concepts.
2. Have a well-established squat 1RM.
3. Have 12+ months of consistent training.
4. Have a minimum of 8 weeks of high recovery capacity (sleep, nutrition).
5. Be able to run 4 squat sessions per week.

## The Base Mesocycle (Weeks 1-4)

Uses percentages of 1RM. Example with 1RM of 400 lbs:

| Week | Set Structure | % of 1RM |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | 4x9, 5x7, 7x5, 10x3 (Mon/Wed/Fri/Sat) | 65/70/75/80 |
| Week 2 | 4x9, 5x7, 7x5, 10x3 (+20 lbs) | 70/75/80/85 |
| Week 3 | 4x9, 5x7, 7x5, 10x3 (+40 lbs) | 75/80/85/90 |
| Week 4 | Repeat Week 3 | — |

## Weekly Structure (Base Mesocycle)

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Monday | Squat 4x9 |
| Tuesday | Recovery |
| Wednesday | Squat 5x7 |
| Thursday | Recovery |
| Friday | Squat 7x5 |
| Saturday | Squat 10x3 |
| Sunday | Full Rest |

## Autoregulation in Practice

Smolov is not autoregulated in the traditional sense. It is prescribed work.
However:

- **If you cannot complete prescribed reps**, drop the load slightly or reduce a set.
- **If readiness is very poor**, skip a session. Do not push through injury risk.
- **Sleep, nutrition, and recovery are non-negotiable.**
- **If two sessions in a row are missed**, restart the phase or scale back.

## Sample Base Mesocycle

With 1RM of 400 lbs:

Week 1:
- Mon: 4x9 at 260
- Wed: 5x7 at 280
- Fri: 7x5 at 300
- Sat: 10x3 at 320

Week 2:
- Mon: 4x9 at 280
- Wed: 5x7 at 300
- Fri: 7x5 at 320
- Sat: 10x3 at 340

Week 3:
- Mon: 4x9 at 300
- Wed: 5x7 at 320
- Fri: 7x5 at 340
- Sat: 10x3 at 360

Week 4: Repeat Week 3.

## When to Switch Programs

Smolov is a tool, not a lifestyle. Switch when:

1. You finish the Base Mesocycle (4 weeks).
2. You finish the full 13 weeks.
3. Your recovery breaks down before completion.
4. You've hit your squat goal.

Return to a general strength program (Wendler, Texas Method, PPL) between
Smolov cycles.

## Common Pitfalls

- Running Smolov without a strong recovery base.
- Attempting Smolov too early in training career.
- Eating insufficiently. Smolov demands calories.
- Trying to run other programs simultaneously.
- Not sleeping 8+ hours per night.
