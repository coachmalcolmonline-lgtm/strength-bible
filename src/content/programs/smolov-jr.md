# Smolov Jr.

Smolov Jr. is a condensed 3-week version of the Smolov squat program. It uses
the same frequency and volume principles but at a shorter duration and can be
applied to Squat, Bench Press, or Press.

It is more sustainable than full Smolov and can be run more frequently.

## Overview

Smolov Jr. runs 4 sessions per week for 3 weeks, using percentages of 1RM.
It is designed to break plateaus and add 10-30 lbs to the specialized lift.

Common applications:

- Smolov Jr. for Squat
- Smolov Jr. for Bench Press
- Smolov Jr. for Press

## Who It Is For

Smolov Jr. is for athletes with 6+ months of training who want a short,
intense specialization block. It is less extreme than full Smolov, allowing
athletes to run it alongside other training.

## Prerequisites

1. Complete all foundational concepts.
2. Have a well-established 1RM on the lift being specialized.
3. Have 6+ months of training experience.
4. Have 3 weeks of high recovery capacity.

## The 3-Week Structure

Uses percentages of 1RM. Example with 1RM of 300 lbs on the specialized lift:

| Week | Day 1 | Day 2 | Day 3 | Day 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | 6x6 @ 70% | 7x5 @ 75% | 8x4 @ 80% | 10x3 @ 85% |
| Week 2 | 6x6 @ 72.5% | 7x5 @ 77.5% | 8x4 @ 82.5% | 10x3 @ 87.5% |
| Week 3 | 6x6 @ 75% | 7x5 @ 80% | 8x4 @ 85% | 10x3 @ 90% |

After 3 weeks, test 1RM (or estimate from a heavy single).

## Weekly Structure

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Monday | Day 1 (6x6) |
| Tuesday | Recovery |
| Wednesday | Day 2 (7x5) |
| Thursday | Recovery |
| Friday | Day 3 (8x4) |
| Saturday | Day 4 (10x3) |
| Sunday | Full Rest |

## Progression Rules

Progress is prescribed. Percentages increase each week. There is no
autoregulation — the program is intentionally rigid to force adaptation.

**If you miss reps:**

1. First miss: reduce weight 5% and continue.
2. Second miss: reduce weight 10% and continue.
3. Third miss: abort the program and rest.

## Autoregulation in Practice

Smolov Jr. is intentionally not autoregulated. But:

- Sleep 8+ hours per night. Non-negotiable.
- Eat enough to support training.
- If sick or injured, abort — do not push through.
- Skip a session if readiness is critical. Restart the week if two sessions missed.

## Sample 3-Week Cycle

With 1RM of 300 lbs:

Week 1:
- Mon: 6x6 @ 210
- Wed: 7x5 @ 225
- Fri: 8x4 @ 240
- Sat: 10x3 @ 255

Week 2:
- Mon: 6x6 @ 217.5
- Wed: 7x5 @ 232.5
- Fri: 8x4 @ 247.5
- Sat: 10x3 @ 262.5

Week 3:
- Mon: 6x6 @ 225
- Wed: 7x5 @ 240
- Fri: 8x4 @ 255
- Sat: 10x3 @ 270

## When to Switch Programs

Move on when:

1. You finish 3 weeks.
2. You test a new 1RM and set a PR.
3. You need a break from squatting.

Return to your regular program.

## Common Pitfalls

- Running Smolov Jr. without a recovery base.
- Trying to run it for multiple lifts simultaneously.
- Not eating enough.
- Not sleeping enough.
- Running it too often (needs 3-4 weeks between cycles).
