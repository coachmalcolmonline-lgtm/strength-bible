# Speed Program

An 8-week speed block that pairs sprint work with heavy strength training and progressive plyometrics. Built on the principle that speed is a neuromuscular quality — trained best with fresh legs, full recovery, and high-quality reps.

## Overview

Two speed sessions per week, two strength sessions per week, one plyometric session integrated into speed days. The block develops acceleration, top-end speed, and the force production that supports both.

**Block structure:**

- Weeks 1–3: Base — establish sprint mechanics, moderate plyometric volume
- Week 4: Deload — half volume, same intensity
- Weeks 5–7: Peak — max-velocity sprints, contrast training, higher plyometric volume
- Week 8: Taper + testing

## Who It Is For

- Field and court sport athletes (football, soccer, basketball, baseball, track)
- Athletes who want to get faster, not just stronger
- Anyone in an off-season or general prep phase
- Athletes who've built a strength base (minimum 6 months)

**Not for:**
- Beginners (build a strength base first)
- Athletes with active hamstring, calf, or Achilles issues
- Anyone mid-season with high game volume

## Weekly Structure

Four sessions per week:

| Day | Session | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Speed A + Plyo | Acceleration, jumps |
| Tuesday | Strength A | Squat, posterior chain |
| Thursday | Speed B + Plyo | Max velocity, bounds |
| Friday | Strength B | Deadlift, upper |

Wednesday, Saturday, Sunday are rest days. Speed days must be separated by at least 48 hours.

## Sample Session — Speed A

**Warm-up (15 min):**
- Jog + dynamic mobility (World's Greatest Stretch, leg swings)
- A-skips, B-skips, high knees, butt kicks — 2×20m each
- Build-up sprints: 3×30m at 70%, 80%, 90%

**Main work:**
1. **Acceleration sprints** — 6×10m from a 2-point or 3-point start. Full recovery (90–120 sec) between reps.
2. **Sled pushes** — 4×15m with moderate load. Full recovery.
3. **Box jumps** — 4×3 to a moderate box. Step down, don't jump down.
4. **Broad jumps** — 3×3. Reset each rep.

**Cooldown:** Walk, light mobility.

## Sample Session — Strength A

1. **Back Squat** — 4×3–5 @ RPE 7–8
2. **Romanian Deadlift** — 3×6–8 @ RPE 7
3. **Hip Thrust** — 3×8–10 @ RPE 8
4. **Nordic Curl** — 3×5–8 (or hamstring curl)
5. **Calf Raise** — 3×12–15
6. **Core** — 3 rounds

## Sample Session — Speed B

**Warm-up (same pattern)**

**Main work:**
1. **Flying 30m sprints** — 4–6 reps. 20m build-up, 30m at max. Full recovery (3–5 min).
2. **Bounds** — 4×30m. Alternating leg bounds for distance.
3. **Depth jumps** — 3×3 from a low box (30–45cm). Soft landing, immediate rebound.
4. **Med ball throws** — 4×3 overhead or rotational.

## Progression Rules

**Sprint volume:**

- Weeks 1–3: 200–300m total per session
- Weeks 5–7: 300–400m total per session
- Week 4 + 8: 50–60% of prior week

**Strength:** Same progression as a linear strength program. Add 5–10 lbs per week on main lifts while keeping RPE 7–8.

**Plyometrics:**

- Weeks 1–3: 40–60 ground contacts per session
- Weeks 5–7: 60–100 ground contacts per session
- Weeks 4 + 8: 20–30 contacts

## Speed Work Rules

These are non-negotiable:

1. **Full recovery between reps.** 90 seconds minimum for acceleration, 3–5 minutes for max velocity.
2. **Stop when speed drops.** If a rep feels slow, end the set. Quality over quantity.
3. **Speed before strength.** Same day → speed first. Different days → speed on fresh legs.
4. **No speed after leg day.** Wait at least 48 hours.
5. **No sprinting on sore hamstrings.** Ever.

## Testing Battery

Test at week 1 and week 8:

- 10m sprint (acceleration)
- 30m sprint (transition)
- Flying 30m (top-end speed)
- Vertical jump
- Broad jump
- 5-10-5 shuttle

Test under identical conditions — same surface, same shoes, same time of day. Meaningful improvements: 0.05s in 10m, 0.1s in 30m, 2cm in vertical jump.

## Nutrition & Recovery

- **Protein:** 1.6–2.2 g/kg per day
- **Carbs:** higher on speed days (fuel for sprint work)
- **Sleep:** 7–9 hours. Speed is the most sleep-sensitive quality.
- **Hydration:** 3+ liters on training days

## Deload Protocol

**Week 4 and week 8:**

- Cut sprint volume 50%
- Keep sprint intensity (all reps still at 90%+)
- Cut plyometric contacts 50%
- Keep strength intensity, cut sets by 30–40%
- No max-effort sprints in week 8 until testing day

## Common Pitfalls

- **Sprinting hard when tired.** This is how hamstrings tear.
- **Too many speed sessions.** Two per week is the max.
- **Skipping warm-up.** Sprinting cold is injury waiting to happen.
- **Chasing times every session.** Save max-effort for testing.
- **Ignoring strength work.** Speed without force production plateaus fast.

## When to Switch

- **After 8 weeks:** retest. Either repeat the block or shift to a maintenance speed program during sport season.
- **In-season:** replace one speed session per week with sport practice.
- **Off-season:** this program stacks well with a strength block.

Uses the **Training Log — Speed** template. Track sprint times, jump heights, RPE, and freshness.
