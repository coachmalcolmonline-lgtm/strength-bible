# Olympic Lift Program

A 12-week block that develops the snatch, clean & jerk, and their derivatives. Built for athletes who want maximum power development -- the highest-transfer training method in the weight room.

## Overview

Olympic lift training is technique-first. This program assumes you already have basic proficiency with the snatch and clean & jerk (or their power versions). If you are brand new to the lifts, spend 4-6 weeks on derivatives and Burgener warm-up progressions before starting this block.

**Block structure:**

- Weeks 1-4: Volume + technique accumulation (moderate intensity, high quality reps)
- Week 5: Deload
- Weeks 6-9: Intensity block (higher loads, lower volume)
- Week 10: Deload
- Weeks 11-12: Peak + test

**Weekly structure:** 4 sessions per week. Two main lifts per session.

## Who It Is For

- Athletes who want maximum power development
- Weightlifters or aspiring weightlifters
- Athletes with a solid strength base (Back Squat 1.5x BW, Front Squat 1.25x BW minimum)
- Athletes with coaching or video feedback

**Not for:**
- Beginners (build strength first with Starting Strength or StrongLifts)
- Athletes without bumper plates or platform access
- Athletes mid-season with high game volume
- Anyone with active shoulder, elbow, or wrist injuries

## Weekly Structure

**Standard 4-day split:**

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Monday | Snatch + Strength (back squat) |
| Tuesday | Clean & Jerk + Accessory |
| Wednesday | Rest / mobility |
| Thursday | Snatch (light) + Clean & Jerk (light) |
| Friday | Strength (front squat) + Derivatives |
| Saturday | Rest |
| Sunday | Rest |

## Block 1 -- Volume Accumulation (Weeks 1-4)

Focus: technique at moderate loads. Volume is the goal -- technique must hold.

**Monday -- Snatch day:**
- Snatch: 6x2 @ 70-75%
- Snatch pull: 4x3 @ 90% of snatch
- Back squat: 4x5 @ 75%
- Overhead squat: 3x5 (mobility work)

**Tuesday -- Clean & Jerk day:**
- Clean & Jerk: 6x(1+1) @ 70-75%
- Clean pull: 4x3 @ 90% of clean
- Front squat: 4x5 @ 75%
- Push press: 3x5 @ 75%

**Thursday -- Technique day (light):**
- Snatch: 5x2 @ 65%
- Clean & Jerk: 5x(1+1) @ 65%
- Snatch balance: 3x5
- Jerk dip: 3x5

**Friday -- Strength + Derivatives:**
- Front squat: 4x5 @ 80%
- Power clean: 5x3 @ 75%
- Power snatch: 4x2 @ 70%
- Clean pull: 4x3 @ 95%

## Block 2 -- Intensity (Weeks 6-9)

Focus: higher loads, lower volume. Technique must hold at heavier weights.

**Monday -- Snatch day:**
- Snatch: 5x1 @ 85%
- Snatch: 3x1 @ 90%
- Snatch pull: 3x2 @ 100% of snatch
- Back squat: 4x3 @ 85%

**Tuesday -- Clean & Jerk day:**
- Clean & Jerk: 5x(1+1) @ 82%
- Clean & Jerk: 3x(1+1) @ 87%
- Clean pull: 3x2 @ 100% of clean
- Front squat: 4x3 @ 85%

**Thursday -- Technique / Speed day:**
- Snatch: 5x1 @ 75% (focus on speed)
- Clean & Jerk: 4x(1+1) @ 75%
- Power clean: 3x3 @ 70%
- Jump shrug: 3x5 @ 40%

**Friday -- Strength + Derivatives:**
- Front squat: 5x2 @ 90%
- Clean pull: 4x2 @ 105%
- Snatch pull: 4x2 @ 105%
- Overhead press: 3x5 @ 80%

## Block 3 -- Peak (Weeks 11-12)

Focus: heavy singles, near-max attempts, low volume, full recovery.

**Week 11:**
- Snatch: 3x1 @ 90%
- Snatch: 1x1 @ 93%
- Clean & Jerk: 3x(1+1) @ 87%
- Clean & Jerk: 1x(1+1) @ 90%
- Back squat: 3x2 @ 90%

**Week 12 (test week):**
- Day 1: Snatch 1RM
- Day 2: rest
- Day 3: Clean & Jerk 1RM
- Day 4: rest
- Day 5: optional back squat / front squat 1RM

## Warm-Up Protocol (Every Session)

Non-negotiable. The Burgener warm-up takes 8-10 minutes and prepares every position.

**Burgener warm-up sequence:**
1. Down and up (dip and drive) -- 5 reps
2. Elbows high and outside -- 5 reps
3. Muscle clean -- 5 reps
4. Muscle snatch -- 5 reps
5. Tall clean -- 5 reps
6. Tall snatch -- 5 reps

Then:
- Empty bar power clean + front squat + press: 2 rounds
- Progressive loading: 40% / 50% / 60% / 70% of working weight
- 2 reps at each warm-up weight

**Full warm-up:** ~15 min before top sets.

## Progression Rules

**Volume block:**
- Add 2.5 kg per week to snatch and clean & jerk
- Add 5 kg per week to squats and pulls
- Keep technique sharp -- if it slips, hold the weight

**Intensity block:**
- Add 2.5-5 kg per week to snatch and clean & jerk
- Squat and pull weights climb to 85-90%
- Reset to technique if bar path degrades

**Peak block:**
- Attempt PRs at week 12
- If a lift fails twice in a row, drop 5 kg and rebuild

## Progression Tests

Test 1RM at week 1 (baseline), week 5 (mid-block), and week 12 (final).

Meaningful improvements:
- Snatch: +5-10 kg over 12 weeks
- Clean & Jerk: +5-15 kg
- Power clean: +5-10 kg
- Vertical jump: +2-4 cm

## Recovery & Nutrition

Olympic lifts are the most CNS-demanding barbell work.

- **Sleep:** 8-9 hours
- **Protein:** 1.8-2.2 g/kg per day
- **Calories:** at maintenance or slight surplus
- **Water:** 3+ liters on training days
- **Rest between sets:** 2-3 minutes minimum for main lifts

## Deload Protocol

**Weeks 5 and 10:**

- Cut main lift volume by 40%
- Keep intensity at 65-75%
- Only technique reps, no max attempts
- Full sleep, full food

## Common Pitfalls

- **Too much volume too fast.** Olympic lifts punish overtrained athletes.
- **Chasing PRs every session.** The block is planned for a reason.
- **Skipping the warm-up.** Cold technique is bad technique.
- **Poor video feedback.** If you can't see your reps, you can't fix your positions.
- **No coaching.** Olympic lifts are the least forgiving of bad habits.
- **Training through pain.** Shoulder, elbow, wrist -- stop and reassess.

## When to Switch

- **After 12 weeks:** retest, then either repeat the block with new 1RMs or transition to a strength or sport-specific block.
- **In-season:** drop to 2x per week maintenance, low volume, high quality.
- **Off-season:** this block stacks well with a hypertrophy or strength block.
