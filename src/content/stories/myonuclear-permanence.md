# The Myonuclear Permanence Hypothesis

When you train, satellite cells donate their nuclei to muscle fibers. These myonuclei are permanent — they don't go away when you detrain. This means that once you've built a foundation of muscle, it's easier to rebuild later. The nuclei are already there, waiting.

This is why athletes who took years off can come back faster than true beginners. The infrastructure remains. The machine remembers.

Progressive overload doesn't just build muscle. It builds the capacity to build muscle.
