# The First Pull-up

Everyone remembers their first pull-up. Not because it was impressive to anyone else — because it was the moment you proved you could do it.

You might have spent weeks doing negatives, band-assisted reps, and dead hangs. You might have failed ten times. Then one day, you pulled. And your chin went over the bar.

That first pull-up changes something. It's the same movement millions of people cannot do. And you just did it.
