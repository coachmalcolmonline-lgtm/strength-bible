# The Two Athletes

Two athletes trained together. Same program. Same numbers.

One day, Athlete A slept 4 hours, ate a donut for breakfast, and was stressed about a test. He went to the gym and tried to hit his programmed numbers. He missed every rep. He walked out frustrated and sore.

Athlete B slept 8 hours, ate a real breakfast, and was calm. She also hit the same programmed numbers — easily. She left feeling strong.

The next week, both athletes were in the same state as Athlete A on that bad day. Athlete A again pushed through. He missed reps again and got hurt.

Athlete B adjusted. She dropped the weight 10%, hit all her reps clean, and finished the session feeling good. The next week, she hit her original programmed weights with room to spare.

Athlete A wondered why he plateaued. Athlete B didn't.
