# Training Log — Power

Living journal for the 8-week power block. Track contrast pairs, jump/throw results, plyometric volume, and freshness. Power is a CNS quality — this log rewards quality over quantity.

## How to Use This Log

1. Log every contrast pair — heavy lift + explosive movement.
2. Log the result of every explosive movement.
3. Track plyometric ground contacts per session.
4. **Rate your freshness before training.** Power on tired legs is wasted work.
5. Weekly reflection every Sunday. Testing weeks 1 and 8.

## Progression Reminders

- **Contrast pairs are the core.** Heavy → rest 3 min → explosive.
- **Never above RPE 8 on heavy sets.** This is not a strength peak.
- **Quality of jump > quantity of jumps.** Stop when a jump is slow.
- **48 hours between hard power sessions.**
- **Deload week 5.** Taper week 8.

## Plyometric Volume Guidelines

| Level | Contacts/Session |
|---|---|
| Beginner | 40–60 |
| Intermediate | 60–80 |
| Advanced | 80–100 (max 150 in peak) |

Depth jumps count as 2–3 contacts each.
