# Training Log — Olympic Lifts

Living journal for the 12-week Olympic lift block. Track snatch and clean & jerk loads, technique quality, and freshness. Quality matters more than weight in this program -- if technique slips, the log tells you first.

## How to Use This Log

1. Log every session -- snatch, clean & jerk, squats, pulls.
2. Rate technique quality (1-10) on each main lift. Be honest.
3. Track freshness before training. Olympic lifts require a fresh CNS.
4. Video at least one set per session. Note the fix.
5. Weekly reflection every Sunday. Test weeks 1, 5, and 12.

## Technique Quality Scale

| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| 10 | Perfect -- positions held, bar path vertical, all reps felt smooth |
| 8-9 | Very good -- minor bar drift or timing, but no misses |
| 6-7 | Acceptable -- some reps slowed, catch was off, but completed |
| 4-5 | Poor -- technical breakdown, one or two misses |
| 1-3 | Bad -- multiple misses, no confidence, stop the session |

If a session is rated below 5 on the main lift, cut the volume and reset.

## Progression Reminders

- **Technique first, load second.** If it doesn't feel right, don't chase it.
- **Volume block (weeks 1-4):** moderate loads, high-quality reps.
- **Intensity block (weeks 6-9):** heavier loads, lower volume, keep positions.
- **Deload weeks 5 and 10.**
- **Test weeks 1, 5, and 12.**

## Warm-Up Checklist (Every Session)

- [ ] Burgener warm-up sequence (down/up, elbows high, muscle clean, muscle snatch, tall clean, tall snatch)
- [ ] Empty bar work: power clean + front squat + press
- [ ] Progressive loading: 40% / 50% / 60% / 70%
- [ ] Full warm-up: 15 min
