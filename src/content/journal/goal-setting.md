# Goal-Setting Worksheet

A dream is "I want to get stronger." A goal is "I will squat 225 lbs for 5 reps by December 1st, training 3× per week, tracking every session."

Dreams are nice. Goals are measurable.

## How to Use This

Pick **one timeframe** and **one goal type**. Fill it out. Then write the milestone dates on your calendar. A goal with no date is a wish.

## Check In

- **Weekly:** are you on pace for the next milestone?
- **Monthly:** did the goal move? If not, why not?
- **End of timeframe:** did you hit it? Reset — new goal, new date.
