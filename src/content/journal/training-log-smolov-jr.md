# Training Log — Smolov Jr.

Living journal for athletes running Smolov Jr. Three weeks. Four sessions per
week. One specialized lift. Three weeks of intensity, then retest.

## How to Use This Log

1. Log each session with day number, weight, sets, reps.
2. Note RPE — should climb through the week.
3. Weekly reflection every Sunday.
4. Test 1RM after week 3.

## Progression Reminders

- Week 1: 70/75/80/85%
- Week 2: 72.5/77.5/82.5/87.5%
- Week 3: 75/80/85/90%
- Test new 1RM at end of week 3.
