# Training Log — Smolov

Living journal for athletes running Smolov. 13 weeks. Three phases. Four
squat sessions per week in Base, three in Intense. Recovery is the priority.

## How to Use This Log

1. Before each session, log readiness.
2. Record squat weight, sets, reps, RPE.
3. Note any missed sets or reps.
4. Weekly reflection every Sunday.
5. Phase reviews at phase boundaries.

## Progression Reminders

- Base: 4 weeks of prescribed work. Add 20 lbs per week.
- Switching: 2 weeks, reduced frequency.
- Intense: 6 weeks, peak intensity.
- Taper: Week 13, rest and retest 1RM.
