## Why This Matters

Motivation fades. Discipline carries you. But discipline needs a foundation — and the foundation is WHY.

The athlete who knows their WHY shows up on the days they don't feel like it. The athlete who doesn't, quits.

Write it down. Read it when you forget. Update it as you grow.

## Revisit Every 90 Days

Your WHY will change. You'll be different at 16 than you are at 18. You'll care about different things. You'll want different outcomes.

Update this page. Track how your WHY evolves.
