## The Big Three

**1. Sleep (7–9 hours)**
The single most important recovery variable. Growth hormone peaks during deep sleep. Muscle repair happens at night. Skip sleep, skip progress.

**2. Nutrition**
Protein 1.6–2.2 g/kg bodyweight. Enough calories to support training and recovery. Water throughout the day.

**3. Stress Management**
Life stress counts as training stress. School, work, relationships, poor sleep — all of it adds to the load. Manage it like you'd manage volume.

## Additional Tools

**Self-Myofascial Release (SMR):** Foam rolling, lacrosse balls, The Stick. 5–15 minutes as needed.
**Mobility Work:** Deep squat holds, World's Greatest Stretch, pass-throughs. 5–15 minutes daily.
**Active Recovery:** Walking, easy biking, swimming. Low-intensity movement helps blood flow.
**Rest Days:** Not optional. Planned. Non-negotiable.

## Adaptive Frequency

**Novice:** Log daily. You're learning what good recovery feels like.
**Intermediate:** Log daily, but focus only on the big three.
**Advanced:** Log when it matters — before high-intensity sessions, during deloads, or when you feel off.

## Journal Integration

Recovery log data feeds the **Weekly Reflection** and **Training Log** readiness markers. If sleep drops below 7 hours for 3+ days, the system suggests a pull-back week.
