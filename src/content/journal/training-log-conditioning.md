# Training Log — Conditioning

Living journal for the 8-week conditioning block. Track session type, duration, heart rate zones, and how your engine responds over time.

## How to Use This Log

1. Log every session -- LISS, tempo, HIIT, sprints.
2. Track heart rate when possible (Zone 2 should feel easy; if you can't talk, you're going too hard).
3. Rate RPE honestly.
4. Track morning resting HR and HRV if you have a wearable.
5. Weekly reflection every Sunday. Test weeks 1 and 8.

## Progression Reminders

- **80/20 rule:** 80% of volume easy, 20% hard.
- **Zone 2 target:** full conversation pace. Slow down if you can't.
- **Full recovery between sprint reps:** 3-5 min.
- **Deload week 5.** Test week 8.
- **Never stack hard conditioning on hard lifting.** 6+ hours apart minimum.

## Heart Rate Zones (Quick Reference)

| Zone | % Max HR | Talk Test | Primary System |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 50-60% | Full conversation | Oxidative |
| 2 | 60-70% | Full conversation, slightly breathless | Oxidative |
| 3 | 70-80% | Short sentences | Mixed |
| 4 | 80-90% | A few words | Glycolytic |
| 5 | 90-100% | No talking | ATP-PC + glycolytic |

Estimate max HR: 220 - age. Not perfect, but close enough for zone training.
