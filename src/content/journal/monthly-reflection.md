# Monthly Reflection

Weekly reflection zooms in. Monthly reflection zooms out. Use this once every 4 weeks — or when the reflection panel nudges you.

## How to Use This

Answer honestly. The point isn't to fill every box — it's to notice patterns you'd miss week to week.

**Novice athletes:** monthly reflection is mandatory. You're still learning what normal feels like.

**Intermediate athletes:** monthly reflection is expected. You know your body — now learn your trends.

**Advanced athletes:** quarterly is often enough. Switch to monthly when you're approaching a meet, a peak, or a stall.
