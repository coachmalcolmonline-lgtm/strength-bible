## Why This Matters

PRs are not the goal. But they're proof the process is working. When you look back at a year of training, you want to see evidence that you've moved forward.

Sometimes the evidence is a heavier bar. Sometimes it's a smoother rep at the same weight. Sometimes it's a first — a movement you couldn't do at all last month.

Write them all down.
