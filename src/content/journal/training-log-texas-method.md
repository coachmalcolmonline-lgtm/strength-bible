# Training Log — Texas Method

Living journal for athletes running Texas Method. Three days per week.
Volume Day, Recovery Day, Intensity Day. One week per cycle. Log every
session and track Intensity Day PRs.

## How to Use This Log

1. Monday — Volume Day. Log all sets and RPE.
2. Wednesday — Recovery Day. Log light work.
3. Friday — Intensity Day. Log PR attempts.
4. Weekly reflection every Sunday.
5. Cycle review every 4 weeks.

## Progression Reminders

- Volume Day: +5/+10 each week.
- Recovery Day: 80% of Volume Day weight. No pushing.
- Intensity Day: attempt new 5-rep maxes weekly.
- Stall = repeat weight once, then reset 10%.
